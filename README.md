## Original Paper
E. Korkmaz and A. P. Akgüngör, "A hybrid traffic controller system based on flower pollination algorithm and type-2 fuzzy logic optimized with crow search algorithm for signalized intersections," *Soft Computing* 28:7227-7249, 2024, doi:10.1007/s00500-024-09643-w.

## Problem Description
At an isolated signalised intersection a fixed-time plan cannot follow random arrivals. The paper lets a fuzzy system lengthen or shorten the next green by up to ±6 s using two detector values (vehicles in the 175 m range, queue length).

## Method
Reproduced part (minimum reproduction requirement): the **Type-1 and interval Type-2 Sugeno green-time controller with CSA-tuned membership functions**, compared with fixed-time control (Webster and FPA/HCM greens), on the 18 demand scenarios (3 and 4 legs) of Tables 9-10 of the paper, 20 test seeds. Not reproduced: FPA phase-plan selection, phase-order optimisation every 3 cycles, left-turn-pocket geometries, the authors' simulator KU-Trsim (not public).

## Fuzzy System Architecture
* Inputs: number of vehicles (0-40, 7 triangular MFs), queue length (0-175 m, 6 MFs); 42 rules `IF count is MFi AND queue is MFj THEN change = z_ij` (min t-norm).
* Output: Sugeno singletons -6, -4, ..., +6 s. The paper does not publish its rule table; the table here is a monotone design of my own (`src/fuzzy_systems.py`).
* IT2: upper MF + lower MF (height 0.5), Karnik-Mendel type reduction (checked against brute force in `tests/`), optional Nie-Tan.
* MF centres and legs are tuned per scenario by CSA (also FPA, PSO, DE in the extension) on 4 training seeds.

## Dataset
No external data. Demand is generated by the simulator from the paper's volumes and a seed (see `data/README.md`).

## Reproduction Experiments
Mean delay (s/veh), 9 scenarios x 20 seeds:

| | Fixed (Webster) | FPA-TSC | Type-1 FL | IT2 FL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3-leg | 36.29 | 36.25 | 33.36 | 33.68 |
| 4-leg | 79.95 | 78.83 | 74.85 | 74.87 |

Tuned fuzzy controllers: about 10 % less delay than fixed time; IT2 vs Type-1: -0.1 % / -0.5 % (paper: +2.7 % / +5.3 %). Details: `results/tables/baseline_*.csv`.

## Proposed Extension
A. Optimiser comparison (CSA, FPA, PSO, DE; equal budget). B. Detector-noise robustness, Type-1 vs IT2. C. Karnik-Mendel vs Nie-Tan type reduction, computational cost, search-budget study.

## Experimental Results (extension)
* CSA 12.35 %, FPA 11.52 %, PSO 11.23 %, DE 12.44 % delay reduction vs uniform MFs; CSA ≈ DE, better than FPA and PSO (Holm p = 0.002 / <0.001).
* Noise up to 75 %: benefit falls from ~10 % to ~4 % for both; IT2 loses slightly less, not significant (p ≥ 0.44).
* Nie-Tan: +0.6 % delay, inference 34.7 vs 51.3 µs; 400 instead of 100 tuning iterations over-fits (6 of 8 cases worse on test seeds).

## Limitations
Mesoscopic own simulator, split phasing (4-leg high demand is over-saturated), no phase-plan selection / left-turn pockets, assumed rule table, 4 training seeds, non-independent scenarios in the Friedman test. See report Section 10.
